# Cipher of The Deep
By: Aaron, Clayton, & Ella
## Game Pitch
Do you enjoy 2D games? Do you like RPGs? Cipher of the Deep is a 2D top-down RPG dungeon crawler where you can play as a knight, mage, or rogue. You’ll fight opposing monsters, explore a sprawling dungeon, and conquer challenging bosses. As you navigate, you’ll find powerful gear to boost your stats and level up to grow strong enough to face the final boss, “The Dark Mage.” This game is designed to challenge your combat knowledge, navigation, and strategy.
## Project Synopsis
In Cipher of the Deep players start off by choosing one of three classes: knight, mage, or rogue. This determines their playstyle and the type of loot players will find in chests, getting armor and weapons that match their class. All enemies slain grant the player experience to level up and gain new skills and attacks, allowing them to grow stronger as they pass through doors and explore deeper levels of the dungeon. Some of these new levels contain bosses that test the player’s strength and skills. Players will traverse even further into the dungeon to try and defeat the final boss, “The Dark Mage,” who lured them into the dungeon and will be very challenging to beat.
## Objective
The main objective of Cipher of the Deep is to defeat the final boss, “The Dark Mage.”
## Mechanics
* **Movement**: WASD for top-down exploration.
* **Combat**: Turn-based encounters triggered on enemy interaction. Players choose among several attacks or defensive options.
* **Progression**: Gain levels and gear by defeating enemies and finding loot throughout chests in the dungeon.
